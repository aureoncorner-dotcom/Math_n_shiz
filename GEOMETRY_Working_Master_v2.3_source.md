## 2 Core geometry (native)

Declare a source domain \(D\), a retained map \(\pi:D\to Q\), a witness \(W:D\to Y\). Work on attained labels \(Q=\pi(D)\).

**Witness-relative equivalence**

\[
x\sim_W y \iff W(x)=W(y),\qquad Q_W \cong \operatorname{im} W.
\]

**Non-descent locus**

\[
N_W(\pi)=\{\,q\in Q:\ \exists\, x,y\in\pi^{-1}(q)\ \text{with}\ W(x)\ne W(y)\,\}.
\]

**Sector space** \(\mathsf S_W(\pi)=\operatorname{im}(\pi,W)\). Canonical refinement \(\pi_W(x)=(\pi(x),W(x))\). Fiber diameter \(\delta_W(q)=\operatorname{diam} W(\pi^{-1}(q))\) when \(Y\) is metric.

**Exact descent**

\[
W=\overline W\circ\pi \iff \operatorname{Eq}(\pi)\subseteq\operatorname{Eq}(W).
\]

**Predictive closure** (deterministic). Forward-invariant domain, update \(U:D\to D\):

\[
\pi(x)=\pi(y)\ \Longrightarrow\ \pi(Ux)=\pi(Uy).
\]

Stochastic: equal next-observation laws on every measurable target when labels agree. A fitted one-trace table does not establish the universal condition.

**No-shape.** Without extra hypotheses, any subset of \(Q\) can occur as \(N_W(\pi)\). A local collision does not determine global geometry.
