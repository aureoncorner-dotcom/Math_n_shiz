Why this is an optimal variance frontier

Fix any mean release time m. Among all releases Y ≥ R with that mean, choose T so Y* = max(R,T) has mean m. Then Var(Y) − Var(Y*) ≥ E[(Y − Y*)²] ≥ 0. The threshold is the unique minimum-variance release, up to events of probability zero.

For each readiness value, Y* minimizes y² − 2Ty over y ≥ R. Taking expectations cancels the equal means and proves the bound. It does not optimize task completion, content errors, interruption rates, or human experience.

Geometry is witness-relative. Finite constructions stay finite. A computed timing benefit is different from a measured user benefit.
