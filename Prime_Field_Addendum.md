## 2. New source-bearing chart witness: PFW

Define the optional witness at a declared observation time `t`:

`PFW_t := (selection_rule, source_digest, point_rows, frame, edge_rule, transform_order, declared_contacts, status, t)`.

- `point_rows` records exact integer source indices and original local coordinates, then any derived floating-point coordinates separately.
- `frame` is one of `grid`, `room`, or `artwork`. Grid uses y-up; artwork uses y-down. A 13-foot room, when explicitly specified, uses feet and its own spatial zero. No frame conversion is inferred from decorations, filename, or symbolism.
- `edge_rule = source_order_open` means a drawn line joins points in ascending source-index order. It is **not** evidence of electrical, acoustic, interpersonal, or platform contact.
- `declared_contacts` is a separate typed relation. Its default is `UNRESOLVED` rather than a guessed connection.
- `status` distinguishes `GENERATED`, `SOURCE_CROSSCHECK_PASSED`, `UNRESOLVED`, and `FAILED`. A renderer's output is never promoted into an empirical witness automatically.

When adopted by an application that already permits chart data, embed this witness in **C** and its source, hash, consent boundary, and chronology in **K**. Do **not** append a fifth coordinate to `F_D` or force this witness to be a universal metric.
