## Read-depth key

- **Deep/full:** full fetched text audited for assigned claims; no automatic certification of historical theorems or empirical counts.
- **Deep/current:** current core/master sections audited; other tabs and historical sections screened.
- **Screened:** full returned text available and screened for structure, scope and relevant geometry passages; no proof or experiment replay.
- **Sheet screen:** fetched workbook text and geometry/evidence sheets inspected; formulas and full native workbook structure not independently audited.

Connector text can omit native equation objects, figures or formatting. “Full fetched text” means the complete returned text snapshot; it is not a guarantee of complete native rendering.
